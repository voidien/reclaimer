## Parked shells take no harm
- The owner's rule: a parked shell cannot be hurt. Only a command that ignores invulnerability reaches one, and it leaves a wreck. Sneak-use scraps your own idle shell into a wreck to loot; a stranger cannot, and a plain use does nothing. Tests: `aParkedShellTakesNoHarmButACommandReachesIt`, `sneakUseScrapsYourOwnShellAndNoOneElses`, both mutation-checked.
