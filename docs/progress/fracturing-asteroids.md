## Fracturing asteroids
- 48 blocks drilled within a 32-block cube split the rock: a slab of up to 48 blocks comes away as a real ship and drifts straight out at 1 to 3 blocks a second, with a crack of sound. At most 12 loose pieces are loaded at once, so ships stay few, and past that rocks stop splitting. The count is lost on a restart, which only means a rock holds out a little longer. Numbers are in config.
- From the T14's review: the first version cut a ball around the hole and pushed it into the rock, so a piece from a pit stayed wedged. It now cuts a slab that has a clear way out, and the test checks that it leaves. If there is no way out the count stands. `RockFractureEvent` lets an addon stop it. The Harvester doesn't count: it takes blocks whole.
