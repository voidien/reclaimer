## Hit boxes: the server hears a body's heading
- The flying client sends where a big body points while it lags its view, and a last word when it catches up (`BodyHeadingPayload`). The server holds that to 1.5 times the body's rate, with no more than 20 ticks counted, and syncs it to everyone but the flyer (`ModAttachments.BODY_HEARD`), so other clients draw it, other bodies stop at it and the box overlay shows it. Test: `theServerHoldsAHeardHeadingToTheBodysRate`.
- The server looks at a big body's boxes every 10 ticks: 0.5 blocks or more into something for 2 seconds sends it back to where it was last clear (`aBodyHeldDeepInAWallGoesBack`), and a body that has never been clear there is left alone, so a chassis fitted in a tunnel isn't punished. Creative bodies are exempt, as from the speed check. The speed check now allows for a turn's pushes, so a thrusterless body turning off a wall is not rolled back.
- From the T14's review: the first word of a turn starts from where the body pointed, not the view, which has already moved by then. That had a quick flick pull a Titan back from a wall it never touched. The client repeats itself once a second while lagging, ships don't count in the overlap check because one can move into a body by itself, and only one step counts per tick.
- **Known issues:** a block placed inside your own boxes, by the Nanite Swarm or a falling Earthfall rock, can cost one pull-back of up to two seconds of flight. Other players see a turning body step at 20 Hz, with no smoothing.
