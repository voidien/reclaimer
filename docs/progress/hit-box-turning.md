## Big bodies turn with weight
- Steps 6 and 7 of `docs/design/hit-boxes.md`. The view turns at once and a Ship, Shuttle or Titan follows as fast as its parts and mass let it (`body/BodyTurning`), thrust goes where the nose points, and a turn into something pushes the body away, then stops. The model and cockpit follow the heading, and a small marker on the HUD shows where the nose is while it catches up. The numbers are a first guess, in the `bodyTurning` config: half a turn takes a Ship about 2.8 s and a Titan about 7.4 s. Test: `aBigBodyTurnsWithWeightAndThrustFollowsTheNose`.
- Half turns are the owner's numbers: Shuttle 1.5 s, Ship 4 s, Titan 12 s, and a heavier load takes longer. `theBuiltInBodiesTakeTheOwnersTimeToTurn` holds them to that; the easing at the end of a turn adds a little more. `/vrphoto handling` shows degrees a second and the time for a half turn.
- From the T14's review: a body moved more than 16 blocks in a tick starts again at its view, and screens that pose a body draw it as they say, not at its heading.
- **Known issues:** the heading lives on the client that flies, so other players and the overlay see the view until step 8. A Helm pilot's ship is unchanged. The model turns about its feet while the cockpit camera starts at the eyes, so at steep pitch the camera leaves the cockpit, about 13 blocks on a Titan at 60 degrees; the view did this before heading too.
