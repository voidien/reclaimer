## Recovering an old shell copy
- The owner's rule: the picker offers it. An old copy that stands refused tells its owner's list where it is, and the picker shows "old copy, recover?" with a second click to confirm. `ShellRecovery` loads the newer copy's chunks first: a shell that is still there is left alone. Otherwise the old copy takes a number above the list's and is the shell; a lost copy that turns up later gives way to it. Tests: `anOldCopyComesBackWhenTheNewerOneIsLost`, `recoveryLeavesAHealthyShellAlone`, `recoveryOfACopyThatHasGoneChangesNothing`, `recoveryAsksForASeatInReachAndNoOutbox`, and the list through a save.
