## Fixes to weather, belt and toxic air
- Fixes from the T14's review: a worn-out section still carries its load and gathers dust at three quarters (health, reach, stability and turn still go), and thrusters at nothing in an old save limp at three quarters instead of stranding the body; a storm already on its way runs out in an empty level, so a link lost to one can be made again; rocks weigh 0.5, so the fastest stops at stone; a wall covers a body right behind it; a rock can't start inside a block; the blocks in `CrashRules.HOLDS_A_CONSTRAINT` are in `crash_proof`, which also protects them from ships; a rock's hit goes by the health under plating.
- Decided alone: the first landing stays manual. The owner's rule is a landing module for the automatic brake (`docs/design/plans.md`), so holding down to the ground at top speed is a crash, and the arrival message now says so. The T14 builds the landing module next.
