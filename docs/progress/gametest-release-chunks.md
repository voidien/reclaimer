## GameTests let go of chunks between batches
- Every forced test area kept about 625 half-made chunks in memory for the whole run, and the remote control tests took a fresh world past 10 GB (66,000 chunks, about 17 million sections). `GameTestBatchChunkMixin` releases all forced chunks as each batch starts, when every test before it has finished. Only in runs that write no chunks: with real saves each unloading chunk is written, and their data filled a 10 GB heap, as an earlier try found. So real-save runs, on `main` and in restart tests, keep their chunks, and `main`'s GameTests now get the 10 GB heap too (they were overriding it).
- It also ends forced chunks carrying over between local runs, an entry under "Ideas for the owner".
