## Big bodies stop at other bodies
- Step 5 of `docs/design/hit-boxes.md`. A big body now hits other players' bodies and shells, a big one by its own boxes and anything smaller by its vanilla box. `BodyCollisionEvent` fires when a body's move is cut short, which crash damage will use. Bodies pushing ships and helper drones steering round big bodies are still to come. Test: `aBigBodyStopsAtOtherBodies`.
- From the T14's review: another body is found by how far the longest chassis reaches, then judged by its real boxes, because a Shuttle used to fly 30 blocks into a Titan's saucer before its vanilla box was in range (`aShuttleStopsAtATitansNoseFarFromItsVanillaBox`). A Drone or Mech now stops at a big body too (`aDroneStopsAtATitansNose`): the design says bodies hit other bodies, and this was the safest reading while the owner was away. One crash is one `BodyCollisionEvent` with the whole speed lost, not two halves over two ticks, which crash damage needs. Ship blocks far from every box are dropped before the box tests, and a ship's scale is read.
- **Known issues:** a moving ship still passes through a body until it meets the body's vanilla box, since the check runs only when the body itself moves. That goes with bodies pushing ships. Pushes under about 3e-4 blocks are dropped by `Entity.move`, and a big body with no thrusters has its pushes rolled back by the speed check until step 8.
