## The debris belt around Earth
- A band of rocks over Earth from y 180 up, thinning with each sector restored and gone on a healed planet. A body now comes out of the portal at y 300 over the return gate and flies down through it, with a message telling it to keep its thrusters powered, since fall damage applies without power. Ships and items still arrive at the gate.
- **Decided alone:** it isn't weather, because it is always there, so it is its own small class on the same rocks. A Drone's safe descent is its own hover: there is no landing module yet.
