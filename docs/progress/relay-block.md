## Relay block, part 1
- The Relay is a block now: it runs on Forge Energy at 20 FE/t from a 2000 FE buffer and goes dark without it, and a level keeps a saved list of its relays. Right-click reads it, and it has a CC peripheral. It does not carry a signal yet: that is the next PR, then keeping its ship loaded, then the holo network map and HUD signal bars. Recipe: 60/45/30/60 dust, 90 s, Starship milestone. `docs/design/plans.md` has the rules.
- **Defaults chosen (safest, reversible):** a dark relay needs a second of energy (400 FE) to restart, so a short supply cycles instead of flipping the block every tick. A row goes only when a loaded chunk shows the relay is not there, so an unloaded relay stays listed as last seen. The readout is on the action bar. The list is not synced to clients: nothing on the client reads it yet.
- **Found by a test:** a ship removed with a relay aboard left its row behind for good, because no block breaks when a ship is removed. Rows now name their ship, and a Sable observer drops them on `REMOVED`, not on `UNLOADED`: `aRelayGoesWithItsShip` and `aShipThatUnloadsKeepsItsRelays`. The restart test's list includes a row aboard a real ship.
- **Fault checks:** 21 faults, all caught. The first pass missed two, which is why `aShipThatUnloadsKeepsItsRelays` exists and `aRelayGoesWithItsShip` ticks the old plot by hand: the restart test cannot see an unload, because the list is saved first, and a removed ship's plot had stopped ticking by itself.
- **Known issues:** `/clone` copies a relay's id, and two blocks with one id share a row. An unloaded relay keeps its last state, so one that lost its power with its chunk still reads lit. Placeholder art: `textures/block/relay.png` and `relay_lit.png`.
