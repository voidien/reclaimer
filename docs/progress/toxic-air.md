## Toxic air and Acid Drizzle are one hazard
- As the owner decided, the drizzle is now one kind in the shared weather system, `toxic_air`, which never stops. It wears every section under the open sky, thinner with each sector restored, and a roof or a ship's hull keeps it off, at 0.01 condition a minute, so 100 minutes outdoors on a fresh body. It never takes health, and `SpaceWeatherEvent.Exposed` is where a filter will stop it. The config `earth.acidWearPerMinute` has gone: the rate is in the file. Weather kinds can now be `always`.
- A section worn right out gives nothing until it is repaired, as the owner chose. **Decided alone:** only the environment can do that, and flying and tools stop at 1%. The last section that can still move a body stops at 1% too, whatever is wearing it: with no thrust in deep space there is no way home and no dust to repair it.
- The research's "likely bug", a body inside a ship on Earth counting as outdoors, isn't one: `AcidDrizzle.underOpenSky` also clips upward, and Sable carries that onto ships.
