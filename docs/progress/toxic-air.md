## Toxic air
- Ruined Earth's air wears every section of a body, under a roof or not, outside restored sectors and thinner with each one restored. At the default a fresh body lasts 100 minutes. It never takes health. `ToxicAirEvent` is where a filter will stop it.
- A section worn right out now gives nothing until it is repaired, as the owner chose, whatever wore it. Thrust wear can do it too, slowly: a body whose thruster sections are all worn out can't fly until it repairs them.
- The research's "likely bug", a body inside a ship on Earth counting as outdoors, isn't one: `AcidDrizzle.underOpenSky` also clips upward, and Sable carries that onto ships.
