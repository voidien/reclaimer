## Remote control: the seat's charge
- While the mind is away the main body gains energy from its seat, faster at a station. It is worked out at the wake from the time the link took, because the main body is not in the world to tick. Config: `link.seatCharge`, `link.stationCharge`. Tests: `aSeatChargesTheMainBody`, `aSeatAtAStationChargesFaster`, `aLinkWithNoSeatChargesNothing`, `SeatChargeTest` (5), mutation-checked.
