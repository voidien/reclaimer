## The Shuttle shell
- `Remote Shell: Shuttle` unfolds into a basic Shuttle, at the Starship milestone like the Ship's. The owner chose it on the ideas page. Prices follow `link.shellPrices`: 490 metallic, 365 silicate, 200 ice and 280 carbon at the default, which is what its parts cost. Item art is the shared placeholder.
- **Known issue:** a Titan shell at parts price would cost 4,172 metallic, and no body holds more than 1,400, so it could not be bought. Its price is the owner's call.
