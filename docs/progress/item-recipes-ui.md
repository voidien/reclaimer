## The Replicator shows what a recipe takes
- The box under the recipe list shows each item a recipe takes and how many you hold, red when short, and Replicate stays off until you have them. JEI shows them too. Test: `heldCountsEverySlot`.
- From the old PC's review: the Output label shows `+N` when a cancel pushes the output past its nine slots, and `link census` counts queued items and the output, so a queue doesn't read as items vanishing.
