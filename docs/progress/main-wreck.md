## A destroyed main body stays as a wreck
- A main body that dies stays where it died as a wreck, with its inventory, armor, offhand, sections, modules and what it paid into the Replicator queue, and the player goes on in a new starter Drone with full energy (`Links.leaveWreck`). The body is in the outbox before it leaves the player, so a crash at any point leaves it in one place: `aMainBodyWreckOutlivesACrash` proves that with a real crash. Nothing falls on the floor. With the keepInventory game rule nothing is left behind, and a body that cannot be saved falls apart as before.
- A wreck that was one when last seen comes back from its outbox as a wreck, for shells too: before, a lost shell came back whole after a crash.
- `MainBodyWreckedEvent` fires once it is done.
- From the T14's review: a body lost in Earth's void leaves its wreck at the bottom of the world, and the void can't kill a wreck or a parked shell, or everything in it was gone for good. A crafting grid open at death goes in the wreck, unless the inventory is full, when it falls. An item from a removed mod stays in a wreck when salvaged.
- **Known issues:** a wreck salvaged in part and then lost to a crash before the first save comes back whole, so the salvaged part is doubled. Shell wrecks do this too. The HUD marker still clears on arrival, and the wreck doesn't drift yet; both come next, with the shell cards on the death screen.
