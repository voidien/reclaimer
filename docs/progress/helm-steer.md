## Helm steering: the mouse turns the ship
- The owner's choice: direct mouse steering by default, with the aim-style controller as a second mode to compare. `ship/AimController` (pure) turns the pilot's keys, mouse and look into a torque. The client sends raw mouse movement (`PilotMouse`). In direct steering the mouse no longer turns the view, which follows the nose every frame (`ShipCamera`); Free Look (Left Alt, held) gives it back, and at the Helm that key does not open the module wheel. A new key, unbound by default, switches modes.
- Keys beat the steering on their axis. The steering also stops a spin, so it replaces the RCS hold's torque. Force and turn go through the allocator together, so a ship that steers with its main engines still turns under full thrust. One empty Helm tick repeats the last mouse movement, because client and server ticks do not line up.
