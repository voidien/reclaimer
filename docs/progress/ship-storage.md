## A Ship holds enough to buy a Titan Core Kit
- The Ship Keel gets one storage mount, on the underside. With Mk II pods a Ship now holds 1,700 of each dust, up from 1,400, which covers the kit's 1,650. The owner chose to raise what a Ship holds on the calls page. Other bodies don't change. `everyCoreCanBeBoughtInASmallerBody` now covers kits too.
