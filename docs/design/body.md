# The body

Settled decisions, moved here from `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them current in the same PR as the change.

**Phase 5a**
- **Parts:** items with a default `part_stats` component. The body (`BodyData` attachment) holds a frame, a core, and per-kind slot lists sized by the frame. Stats are computed purely in `BodyStats`.
- **Size classes:** Drone 0.4 / Mech 1.2 / Ship 3.0 (`generic.scale`). The frame also sets max health, reach, dust intake, base inventory rows, mass budget and slot counts.
- **Mass is a soft limit** (handling and energy penalty over budget). **Tier gate:** part tier ≤ core tier.
- **Inventory:** hotbar + frame base rows + storage rows (max 36). Locked slots take nothing new but can be emptied; creative is exempt.
- **Modules:** sensor and tool modules work only when installed. Tools are used with an empty main hand; the module wheel (hold Left Alt) picks the active one. Nanite: right-click space, or sneak + right-click a block.
- **Install UI:** the Body tab in the Replicator (5b adds the 3D assembly view).
- **Cosmetics:** casing material + accent tint + thruster trail (drawn now as particles), plus cosmetic part slots (drawn in 5b).
- **Mech frame and Mk II parts** unlock with the Expansion milestone (any dust kind at capacity). The Ship frame waits for Phase 6 materials.

**Body sections (after 5a, replaces frames and part slots)**
- A body is a tree of **sections** in **sockets**. The core section names a `chassis` (datapack) that lists the sockets: accepted socket types, `required`, `parent`, `pivot`, `area`. Only the core is required; a missing section empties its child sockets.
- Sections offer **mounts** for modules (core, thruster, storage, sensor, tool, cosmetic). Size class, scale, health, reach and mass budget come from the chassis and its sections.
- Kits for the starting set of a chassis (Drone Kit; Mech Core Kit = torso + pelvis + head); limbs are crafted singly. Mk II sections replace single sockets.
- Old frame/part saves migrate on load; the body is kept on death. Body logic takes a `LivingEntity` where it can (entity-agnostic for helper drones later).
- **Paint:** body default casing + accent, with optional per-section overrides.
- **Wear:** light and repairable. Below 50% condition, efficiency drops up to 25%. Repair costs about 20% of the recipe dust. Never breaks; no wear in creative.
- **Body Blueprint:** save the body, apply it from the inventory, queue Replicator jobs for missing parts.
- **Replicator recipes** may have several `results` and a `title`; a job waits until the buffer fits all of its items. Salvage prices single-item recipes first.

**Test Starship (placeholder entity, 2026-10-29)**
- `void_reclaimer:test_starship` is for checking RD models and animations in game; it is not gameplay. Its model is the owner's RD starship, promoted with `--promote-as entity/starship --scale-like body/ship`. It is drawn at the Ship size class's scale.
- It drifts and turns round its anchor (`StarshipDrift`, from game time, the same on both sides), loops `gear_cycle`, and plays `ignite_prep` on right-click.
- To replace it, promote a new RD `.bbmodel` to the same target. Spawn it from the creative item, with `/void_reclaimer starship spawn`, or with `/summon`.

**Assembly view (built from "Round two")**
- `client/screen/AssemblyScreen`, opened by the 3D view button on the Body tab or the unbound `key.void_reclaimer.assembly`. Uses `BodyPanel` with a `Layout` (the tree left, details right, the model between). `body/assembly/AssemblyCamera` (pure) holds yaw, pitch, zoom, the eased focus and the grid step. Markers come from `BoneProbe` in the GUI pass, where a point's z is depth: `AssemblyMarkers` (pure) picks what shows and what a click hits. Clicking a marker selects it in `BodyPanel` and eases the view to it; clicking empty space clears both. Hovering a part that fits shows a ghost (`BodyRenderer.setPreview`) and its stat changes (`body/assembly/StatDelta`). Exploded view: `BodyRenderer.setExplode`, spreading socket bones in `renderRecursively`. Shift-click fits a pair (`body/assembly/BodyPairs`). Wear tints through `Wear.tint`, and a held Blueprint marks what it wants changed (`Blueprint.differsFrom`).

**Phase 5b-1: the body on the model, third person (2026-10-29, defaults)**
- **Scale trap (fixed 2026-10-31):** `GeoReplacedEntityRenderer.actuallyRender` applies the entity's `getScale()` and `applyRotations` on every pass, re-renders included. So never call `withScale` with the entity's scale here (that squares it), and `BodyRenderer.applyRotations` undoes the scale and skips the turn during a re-render (a part drawn at a bone is already in the body's space). Photo mode's debug panel prints the matrix scale of the body and of its parts: they must equal the body's scale.
- **What it does:** `RenderPlayerEvent.Pre` is cancelled and the player is drawn by `client/render/body/BodyRenderer` (a GeckoLib `GeoReplacedEntityRenderer<Player>` with one shared `BodyAnimatable`). The body is drawn at `generic.scale`, facing where the player looks (flyers also pitch with the view; the upright Mech only turns). The `body` attachment now syncs to everyone who can see the player, not only the owner. Not here: first person (the vanilla hand still shows) and the 3D assembly view, both the next 5b PR.
- **Composed rigs (Drone, Ship; the art's own contract):**
  - `geo/body/<rig>.geo.json` is the core section plus empty marker bones named after the chassis sockets (socket id = bone name). Each installed section's model (`geo/section/<item>.geo.json`) is drawn at its socket bone's pivot; each module (`geo/module/<item>.geo.json`) at the rig bone `mount_<kind>_<n>` or `<socket>__mount_<kind>_<n>`, or else at the same `mount_*` bone inside its section's model. Items without a model are not drawn.
  - `cap_*` bones plug empty sockets and are hidden once the socket has a section.
  - Paint: the top-left 16x16 of every art texture is the casing swatch; `BodyTextures` paints the chosen casing's Create texture into it (a dynamic texture per art texture and casing, cleared on resource reload). The `_accent` mask is drawn again, tinted with the accent colour at full brightness (so lights and trim glow). Per-section paint overrides apply to that section and its modules.
  - Sections are drawn upright at their socket pivot; a section for a mirrored socket is not mirrored yet (art sections are symmetrical or single).
- **Whole-model rigs (the Mech):** `assets/void_reclaimer/body_rigs/<rig>.json` (`BodyRigSpec`) says a rig is one RD model with its own bones and animations: `model`, `texture`, `animations`, `animation_prefix`, `grounded` (origin at the feet), `sockets` (socket id to bones hidden, with their children, while that socket has no section) and `poses` (pose key to animation name). A rig with a spec is not composed: no section models, modules, casing or accent. Pose keys: `idle`, `thrust`, `brake`, `back`, `strafe_left`, `strafe_right`, `low_power`, `module_work`; a missing key falls back to `thrust` (strafe and back) and then `idle`.
  - The Mech is the owner's RD "blocky mech suit", promoted with `--promote-as entity/mech_suit --scale 0.39` (28.8 px, a 1.8-block body at scale 1). Its animations (the owner's re-rigged model, imported 2026-10-30): `idle_ambient` = idle, `idle_impatient` = `idle_long` (the idle variant: after 20 s of standing still the body fidgets for 20 s, then back, and so on), `zoom_flight` = thrust, `fly_back` = back, `fly_left`/`fly_right` = strafes, `swing_tool` = module work. Its `menace_walk` and `menace_run` are not used (no ground in deep space), and it has no low-power animation (it falls back to idle).
  - To replace it: `python3 tools/art/rd_generate.py --promote-as entity/mech_suit --source NEW.bbmodel --scale 0.39`, and keep the group names in the spec (or edit `body_rigs/mech.json`). `BodyRigSpecTest` checks that every socket, bone and animation the spec names exists.
- **Animation:** `BodyLook.pose` picks the pose from the player's motion each tick (speed change for thrust and brake, the part of the step along and across the facing for back and strafe; low power from the energy state; a pose is held 4 ticks). While the player swings, `module_work` plays. Remote players are animated from their movement alone.
- **Camera:** for bodies above the Mech's size the third-person camera stands `CAMERA_FIT` (3) model radii back, measured from the real model (`BodyRenderEvents.cameraDistance`, `BodyBounds`), so each fills about the same share of the screen. Vanilla multiplies the distance by the body scale after the event, so it works in the model's own size. Drone and Mech keep vanilla's distance.
- **Debug commands:** `/void_reclaimer body preset <player> drone|mech|ship [full]` (a complete body: every socket filled, Mk I core, thrusters and storage; the Mech gets a rotor arm jetpack on its back; `full` adds a Mk II core, Mk I and Mk II storage pods in turn, Dust Sensors and the four tool modules, so every module model shows) and `/void_reclaimer body remove <player> <socket>`.
- **Known limits:** the art's textures are very dark (black frames with small casing and accent areas), so the body is drawn with a minimum light (`body.minLight`); the Drone and Ship still look sparse. Modules without models (sensors, storage, core, tools) are invisible. The nameplate and held items are not drawn on the body.

**Velocity lean (2026-10-30, owner's choice: the Mech first)**
- A rig spec may have `lean` (`pitch`, `roll` degrees, `reference_speed` blocks per tick; default none). `BodyLean` (pure) eases the body toward a forward lean (tipping back when flying backward) and a bank into a strafe, about the middle of the body, so one flying animation covers every direction; `BodyRenderer.applyRotations` draws it. The Mech has 22 / 16 / 0.4. Other rigs are off for now.
- The owner's longer-term goal: ships that travel freely in every direction and face every orientation (roll and full 6-DOF). The lean is the first step; nothing else is built.

**Health bar (2026-10-30, owner's choice)**
- One bar replaces the vanilla hearts (`client/hud/HealthBarLayer`, sizes in `HealthBarMath`): longer and taller with max health on a log scale (60 px at 20 health up to the hotbar's 182 px at 1000, height 5 to 9), a notch every 100 health when there is room, and "health / max" above it. Drawn with `GuiGraphics.fill` only.

**Layered animation poses (2026-10-30, owner's request)**
- A rig spec may have `layers`: layer name to (pose key to animation), at most 3, in name order. `BodyAnimatable` registers three extra GeckoLib controllers after the main one; a layer animation only moves the bones it keys, the rest stays with the main animation. A rig with a layer for `module_work` no longer swings its whole body for tool work. The Mech has one: `swing_tool_arms` (`swing_tool` cut to `arm_r` and its descendants with `tools/art/anim_slice.py`) on layer `tool_arm`.
**Animation reuse (2026-10-30, owner's request)**
- `tools/art/anim_slice.py` cuts, mirrors, retimes and bone-filters an animation into a new one (see the art README); layered poses in the rig spec, which play such a part on top of the main animation, come next.

**Cameras (2026-10-30, owner's choices)**
- **Orbit camera:** in third person, hold the orbit key (`key.void_reclaimer.orbit_camera`, middle mouse by default) and move the mouse to look round the body without turning it; scroll zooms (15% per step, 0.25x to 6x); releasing eases the camera back behind the body (`client/camera/OrbitState` pure and tested, `OrbitCamera` controller). The angles are added in `ViewportEvent.ComputeCameraAngles`, which NeoForge fires before the third-person camera is moved back along its rotation, so the camera swings round the body (no mixin needed). 
- **Photo mode** (`client/camera/PhotoMode`, `PhotoModeScreen`, `PhotoState` pure and tested; key `photo_mode`, default F8): detaches the camera from the body, hides the HUD and keeps the game running. Inside: drag with the left mouse button to look, WASD and Space/Shift to move (Ctrl for fast), scroll for the field of view, Q and E to roll (R resets), Enter or F2 for a screenshot, Escape or F8 to leave (the HUD comes back). The camera stays within 256 blocks of the body. Roll and field of view use `ViewportEvent`; the position needs `CameraPhotoMixin` (a small client-only mixin after `Camera.setup`), because no NeoForge event moves the camera. Its debug tools are under "Debug tools".

**First person in big ships (2026-10-30, defaults from the interview decisions)**
- A chassis may have a `cockpit` (see the extension table). While the local player's chassis has one, first person has no hand (`FirstPersonCockpit`, `RenderHandEvent`) and `CameraPhotoMixin` moves the camera from the eyes to the cockpit, scaled with the body. Built in (worked out from the rigs' bridge and windshield socket pivots, in blocks at scale 1, with the eyes 1.62 above the feet, so not yet checked by eye): Ship forward 0.9, up -0.3 (its bridge is 0.75 ahead of and 0.35 below the eyes); Shuttle forward 2.0, up 0 (the windshield is 2.2 ahead); Titan forward 1.7, up 0.5 (the bridge). Tune them in `BuiltinBodies` and run `runData`. Client config `cockpitCamera` (default on) switches it off. Drone and Mech keep the vanilla hand until their first-person pieces exist.

**Titan size class, Core Mk III and IV, whole-rig mounts (2026-10-29, defaults)**
- **Titan:** a fourth size class (`titan`, scale 8, rank 3). The rig is 8.4 blocks long at scale 1, so the body is about 67 blocks long (the owner wants more than 50). Chassis `titan`, core socket `saucer`; sockets (ids are the art rig's bones): `neck`, `bridge`, `sensor` and `deflector`/`bay_door`/`pylon_l`/`pylon_r` with `nacelle_l`/`nacelle_r` under the pylons. Tier-4 section items `titan_saucer` (core section), `titan_neck`, `titan_bridge`, `titan_sensor_array`, `titan_deflector`, `titan_bay_door`, `titan_pylon` and `titan_nacelle`, with mounts: saucer core 1, storage 2, cosmetic 1; bridge sensor 1; sensor array sensor 2; bay door storage 2; nacelle thruster 3.
- **Titan rig:** whole-model rig spec `body_rigs/titan.json` over the owner's RD ship (`geo/entity/titan`): `warp_cruise` is idle and thrust, `bay_open` is not used yet (no docking), `body_rotate` and `bridge_spin` were left out. The model's front is +Z, so `yaw` is 180; it `pitches` with the view like a Ship.
- **Titan prices:** about ten times the Ship's (saucer 1200/900/600/800 dust, 15 minutes; a Titan Core Kit saucer + neck + bridge). Unlock: `TITAN_UNLOCK` in `ModRecipeProvider` is Dyson tier 3 as a placeholder; the owner chooses between the Starship milestone and a Dyson tier later (one constant).
- **Core Mk III and Mk IV** (tier 3 and 4; energy 500/1000, solar 2.0/3.0): a section can't be above the core module's tier, but the best core was Mk II, so Ship sections (tier 3) could not be installed at all. Mk III is unlocked with the Starship milestone, Mk IV with the Titan. Placeholder item icons, no module models yet (they are invisible on a body). `BodyRules.TIER_TOO_HIGH` is tested for both.
- **Whole-model rig specs grew** (`BodyRigSpec`): `yaw` (degrees), `pitches`, `socket_parts` (hide only a bone's own cubes, for a neck that carries the head) and `mounts` (rig mount name to bone, offset in pixels and yaw; offsets use the axes of the pivots in the `.geo.json`). Modules on a whole-model rig are drawn at these mounts; a mount with no entry is not drawn. The Mech got its mounts this way and is turned 180 degrees (its model faces +Z).
- **Camera:** for bodies above the Mech's size the third-person camera stands `CAMERA_FIT` (3) model radii back, measured from the real model (`BodyRenderEvents.cameraDistance`, `BodyBounds`), so each fills about the same share of the screen. Vanilla multiplies the distance by the body scale after the event, so it works in the model's own size. Drone and Mech keep vanilla's distance.
- **Debug:** `/void_reclaimer body preset <player> drone|mech|ship|titan [full]`; the Ship and Titan presets carry the Mk III and Mk IV cores.
- **Not here:** Titan module models (the module art is 4 px, so it would be small at scale 8, but visible), a way to play `bay_open`, Titan-only materials, first person.

**Shuttle layout and the probe skin on the Drone (2026-10-29, owner's choices)**
- **Shuttle:** a second chassis next to the Ship, in its own size class at the Ship's rank (scale 2 since 2026-10-31, so the model is about 17 blocks long). Chassis `shuttle`, core socket `body`; sockets (ids are the art rig's bones): `nose`, `windshield`, `dome`, `ridge`, `aft`, `strut_l`, `strut_l2`, `strut_r`, `strut_r2`, `nacelle_l`, `nacelle_r`. Tier-3 section items `shuttle_hull` (core section, names chassis `shuttle`), `shuttle_nose`, `shuttle_windshield`, `shuttle_dome`, `shuttle_ridge`, `shuttle_aft`, `shuttle_strut` and `shuttle_nacelle`. Which core section you install decides the layout (keel = Ship, hull = Shuttle); both need a Core Mk III. Recipes cost a little less than the Ship's, unlock with the Starship milestone, and there is a Shuttle Core Kit (hull, windshield, nose).
- **Shuttle rig:** whole-model spec `body_rigs/shuttle.json` over the owner's RD shuttlecraft (`geo/entity/shuttle`); `engines_firing` is the thrust animation, there is no idle animation (it hovers still), `hatch_open` and `hatch_ramp` are not used yet. Sockets hide their bones (an empty nacelle socket also hides its exhaust and flames).
- **Drone skin:** `body_rigs/drone.json` draws the Drone as the owner's RD mining probe (`geo/entity/mining_probe`, a hull with a drill and two arms). It is a skin: the Drone's sections (rotor arms, sensor dome, claw) still work but do not change what you see; modules draw at spec mounts (core on top, storage at the back, a sensor at the nose, four thrusters, the tool at the drill). The probe came without animations, so `animations/entity/mining_probe.animation.json` is **placeholder** (a slow hover bob with swaying arms for idle, the arms swept back for thrust). The probe is also the owner's model for the future helper drones (Phase 8).
- **Spec option `offset_y`** (pixels, at the body's scale) moves a centred model up or down; the probe and the shuttle use half their height so the model's middle sits at the hitbox's.
- **Station model:** the owner's RD ring station (`geo/entity/station`, no animations, about 14 blocks across) is stored for Phase 8 (stations the player builds or unlocks); nothing uses it yet.
- **Debug:** `/void_reclaimer body preset <player> shuttle [full]`.
