# Ships, stations and addons

Settled decisions, moved here from `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them current in the same PR as the change.

**Create (decided after 4b)**
- Create is a required dependency. Bodies, cosmetics, machines and stations use Create's look: andesite/brass/copper casings, cogwheels and shafts. Our models may reference Create's textures (`create:block/...`) from the player's installed Create; never copy Create's assets into our jar (its `assets/` are All Rights Reserved; its code is MIT).
- Gear animation in Create style: cogs spin with thrust and speed, with module work (Replicator, drill, nanites), with energy level (low power winds down), and idle slowly.
- Body colors: a casing material (Andesite, Brass, Copper, later unlocks) plus a free accent tint.
- Phases 7–8 build on Create's machines and logistics instead of duplicating them.
- **Create Aeronautics: required (decided 2026-10-29; spike results in `AERONAUTICS_SPIKE.md` on branch `claude/aeronautics-spike`, never merged).**
  - Sable works in `deep_space` with zero physics gravity via `data/<ns>/dimension_physics/*.json` (`base_gravity`, `base_pressure`, `universal_drag`). The first spike's "flung away" ships were falling: the zero-G file wasn't in effect. Height and f32 precision were not factors.
  - Aeronautics propellers and balloons need air, so they give no thrust in vacuum. Our own **Space Thruster** block pushes ships through Sable's force API (Create rotation plus dust fuel).
  - The Ship class stays the player's body; a **Helm** block lets the body pilot a block ship with WASD thrust controls. Block ships serve as mobile stations, cargo haulers, mining rigs, and can cross the Atmospheric Portal (air on Ruined Earth makes propellers work).
  - Aeronautics and Sable are required dependencies (`neoforge.mods.toml`); they are `localRuntime` + `testRuntimeOnly`, so our code doesn't compile against them yet. Add them as `implementation` when the first code needs their API.
  - `data/void_reclaimer/dimension_physics/deep_space.json` gives deep space zero physics gravity, pressure and drag. `AeronauticsGameTests` (own namespace `void_reclaimer_aero`, run `./gradlew runGameTestAeronautics`, own CI job) checks a platform stays put and a propeller gives no thrust in vacuum.
  - Upstream bug: an intermittent start-up deadlock between Aeronautics/Simulated/Veil and Create's render classes in client-dist runs (game, `runData`). Required Aeronautics means `runData` loads it too, so CI wraps `runData` in a 5-minute timeout with up to 3 attempts.
  - `sable64bit` (Chelovechic, AGPL, f64 Rapier) works with Sable 2.0.6 and keeps physics exact far from the origin; recommend it, don't depend on it.
- **Ships and stations without Aeronautics:**
  - Drone, Mech and Ship are all the player's body (GeckoLib, Create style). The Ship is the 3.0-scale class: a mobile base with more storage, module slots and a faster Replicator; it docks at stations and is the class that survives the Atmospheric Portal (Phase 9).
  - Stations are fixed block structures: Create machines and belts inside, Create contraptions (bearings, pistons, gantries) for moving parts such as rotating rings, our controller blocks for multiblock bonuses (Phase 8). Between stations: helper drones and Create trains on tracks laid through space.
  - Keep a block-built Ship and movable stations possible as optional Aeronautics support later: don't design station controllers or a Ship Core in ways that rule it out.

**Create, Aeronautics and addon integration (2026-10-30 interview, owner's answers; nothing built yet)**
- **Themes, all four wanted:** airships on ruined Earth (propellers and balloons work in air: travel between Spire sectors, carry Spire inputs); space ships in deep space (Space Thruster and Helm, haulers and mining rigs); moving stations (Sable sub-level rings, docking, gantries); block ships through the Atmospheric Portal (Sable has no built-in dimension travel; a third-party mod does it by saving and recreating the ship, so this needs its own spike).
- **Body and ships:** vehicles first, brain later. The Drone, Mech and Ship stay the player's body; a block ship is a vehicle flown from a Helm seat. Docking the body into a ship core (the ship inherits its modules, storage, energy and Replicator) is a later phase: keep the data model open for it, and do not hard-code vehicle assumptions into body, parts or Sable code.
- **Addon policy:** a few key addons will be **required** (like Create and Aeronautics: `localRuntime` + `testRuntimeOnly`, `implementation` only when code needs the API); everything else is **optional soft compat** through events, tags and datapack hooks, never compiled against. Which addons become required is decided by a **compatibility spike** (a never-merged branch: load every candidate beside our mod in the client, the GameTests and datagen, record crashes and conflicts, promote only what passes).
- **Candidates (versions checked on Modrinth for 1.21.1 NeoForge, 2026-10-30; Modrinth lists no Create ranges, so every one is unproven):** Create Crafts & Additions (`createaddition`, 1.7.2, beta; electricity), Create: New Age (`create-new-age`, 1.2.0, release; advanced tech), CC: Tweaked (`cc-tweaked`, 1.120.2, alpha; computers), Create: Avionics (`create-avionics`, 0.7.0, alpha; CC for Aeronautics, needs CC: Tweaked), Create Propulsion: Simulated (`create-propulsion-simulated`, 1.1.5; thrusters), Create: Aeroworks (`create-aeroworks`, 1.5.0; aero parts), Steam 'n' Rails (`create-steam-n-rails-1.21.1`, 0.3.0-beta.2; trains). Also small bridges: `create-crafts-additions-and-aeronautics-compat`, `create-additions-sable-compat`. Expected outcome of the spike: required = Crafts & Additions, New Age, CC: Tweaked; optional = Propulsion, Aeroworks, Avionics, Steam 'n' Rails. No combat addons (the vision has no combat).
- **Thrusters:** our Space Thruster is the guaranteed engine (dust fuel and energy); ships using a third-party thruster addon's blocks also work in deep space through the same Sable force path. No player is locked into one engine.
- **Order after hover flight:** compatibility spike, then addon policy and an electricity bridge (Crafts & Additions power into our energy and `CollectorPowerEvent`), then 7b ships (Space Thruster, Helm, third-party thrusters), then airships on ruined Earth, then moving stations, then portal-crossing ships (spike first), then CC: Tweaked peripherals for our machines. The launch complex and helper drones follow. Each step is split into small PRs and gets its own interview before code. **Status 2026-10-31:** everything in this order is done except the owner's airship kit (airships stay Create-flown; interview first). The owner reordered what follows: see "Next four stages".
- **Still open:** what Steam 'n' Rails is used for, and the power units between Create, our energy and the addons.

**Required addons (decided 2026-10-30 by the owner, after the compatibility spike)**
- **Required:** Create Crafts & Additions, Create: Advanced Electricity / Technology Addon, Create: Advanced Computer Technic (the "electronics stack"; the last two need Crafts & Additions), Create Propulsion: Simulated (ship thrusters), Create: The Factory Must Grow (oil, fuels, steel). Same rules as Create and Aeronautics: `localRuntime` + `testRuntimeOnly`, versions and ranges in `gradle.properties`, `required` in `neoforge.mods.toml`; switch one to `implementation` only when our code needs its API.
- **Optional with soft compat:** everything else from the spike (New Age, CC: Tweaked, Create: Avionics, Aeroworks, Steam 'n' Rails, Electro Energetics, Ore Excavation, Power Loader, Hypertubes, Tweaked Controllers, Trading Floor, Numismatics, Connected, Copycats+, Deco). We support them through standard hooks (Forge Energy, tags, events), never by compiling against them.
- **Not usable now:** Create: Power Grid (crashes with TFMG 1.2.0: its TFMG integration needs 1.3.x), Create: Radars (hard-requires Create Big Cannons; no combat in this game). Create: Avionics only works with CC: Tweaked (hard dependency), not with Advanced Computer Technic's computers.
- **Distribution:** two required addons are CurseForge-only (one All Rights Reserved), so a Modrinth modpack cannot bundle them; players need the CurseForge app or a manual download.
- **Risk:** Advanced Electricity and Computer Technic were first released for 1.21.1 on 2026-10-29 with a few hundred downloads; expect updates and breakage, and re-run the GameTests when they change.

**CC: Tweaked peripherals (decided 2026-10-30 by the owner; standing rule)**
- **Every new block or item with state worth reading or an action worth taking gets a CC: Tweaked peripheral API in the same PR** (a generic peripheral in `compat/cc/`, registered in `CCCompat.Sources.all()`, with a row in `docs/CC_PERIPHERALS.md` and a test in `CCPeripheralGameTests`). Existing blocks are being covered in four PRs: factory machines (done), the Dyson launch chain (done, read-only), the Terraforming Spire (done, with `pulse()`), and the Backup Core, Tether Anchor and portal frame (done, read-only). **All existing blocks with state now have peripherals.** Blocks without a block entity use an `IPeripheral` registered on CC's block capability (`CCCompat.Sources.registerCapabilities`). Item upgrades (turtle and pocket computer, e.g. a Mechanical Drill turtle, a Dust Sensor pocket computer) come after.
- **Optional like JEI:** `compileOnly` against CC's API (`cc.tweaked:cc-tweaked-1.21.1-*-api` from `maven.squiddev.cc`), the full mod on `localRuntime` (dev runs and GameTests), `optional` in `neoforge.mods.toml`. Only `CCCompat.onCommonSetup` runs without CC; it checks `isLoaded()` before touching anything else in the package.
- **Generic peripherals:** a class implementing `GenericPeripheral` whose `@LuaFunction(mainThread = true)` methods take our block entity as the first argument. No changes to our block classes; CC attaches them to computers next to the block or on wired modems.
- **Control follows player rules:** a computer can do what a player can by right-click or redstone, through the same code paths and checks, never more.
- **Ship scripting (owner's route 3):** Create: Avionics with CC: Tweaked (both optional). Advanced Computer Technic's CogScript has no extension API (hard-coded functions, a single device slot), so we do not patch it.

**7b block ships (interview 2026-10-30, owner's answers; not built yet)**
- **Thrusters, three blocks:** **Chemical Thruster** (Create rotation plus fuel: high thrust, burns fast), **Ion Thruster** (Forge Energy only: low thrust, efficient), and an **RCS block** for fine rotation (our existing `rcs_thruster` item becomes placeable, keeping its item id so saves and recipes stay valid).
- **How they push (checked in Sable 2.0.6's jar):** the thruster block entity implements Sable's `BlockEntitySubLevelActor`; in `sable$physicsTick(ServerSubLevel, RigidBodyHandle, double dt)` it applies an impulse at its nozzle (`RigidBodyHandle.applyImpulseAtPoint`, also `applyLinearImpulse`, `applyTorqueImpulse`, `getLinearVelocity`). Placement therefore matters: an off-centre thruster turns the ship. Look at how `BlockEntitySubLevelPropellerActor.applyForces` converts local thrust to an impulse (javap -c) before writing ours; Sable must become a `compileOnly` dependency (it is already required at runtime).
- **Activation before the Helm:** redstone signal strength 0-15 is the throttle (works with Create links and contraptions; stays useful after the Helm).
- **Chemical fuel:** Nebulite Propellant (ours, strongest) plus TFMG fuels (diesel, kerosene, gasoline, LPG; verify their fluid ids in the TFMG jar) at their own strengths, through a `void_reclaimer:thruster_fuel` data map (fluid -> thrust, burn rate) so packs can add more.
- **Helm: full 6-DOF** (pitch, yaw, roll and translation) - the hardest part; it gets its own short interview on controls and camera before any code.
- **Create Propulsion (required) in vacuum: done, no bridge needed.** Its thrusters scale their push by Sable's air pressure (`AtmosphericThrustMath`: full at zero pressure, a fifth at sea level with the default config), and `dimension_physics/deep_space.json` already sets `base_pressure` 0, so a powered Propulsion thruster pushes a ship through deep space at full strength; `aCreatePropulsionThrusterPushesItsShipInVacuum` (a redstone-powered `createpropulsion:creative_thruster`) keeps it that way. Propulsion's thrusters are plain `BlockEntitySubLevelActor`s like ours, controlled by redstone (0-15) or a scroll value; they are not `ThrusterLike`, so the Helm does not command them (a `ThrusterLike` adapter for them is a possible later step).
- **Done, PR 1:** `ship/ChemicalThrusterBlock` and `ChemicalThrusterBlockEntity` (a `BlockEntitySubLevelActor`; the server tick decides `force`, `sable$physicsTick` queues it on `ForceGroups.PROPULSION` in the ship's own coordinates at the block, scaled by `dt`, like Sable's propeller), `ThrusterMath`, `api.ship.ThrusterFuel` and the data map `void_reclaimer:thruster_fuel` (`data_maps/fluid/thruster_fuel.json`, server only). Sable is `compileOnly`; `extractCreateLibs` also unpacks its nested Veil and companion jars.
- **Done, PR 2:** `ship/IonThrusterBlock` and `IonThrusterBlockEntity` (a plain block, not kinetic: no shaft and no fuel, a receive-only Forge Energy buffer; a short buffer gives a proportional push, `ThrusterMath.ionDraw`). All thrusters queue their push through `ship/ThrusterPush`.
- **Done, PR 3:** the RCS block: `ship/RcsThrusterBlock` and `RcsThrusterBlockEntity`, a small jet on Forge Energy (the owner left its power open; decided unattended, see `PROGRESS.md`). `ship/ElectricThrusterBlockEntity` is the base of the Ion and RCS thrusters; subclasses only set the numbers. The `rcs_thruster` item keeps its id, stays in `ModMaterials.ALL` and is now a block item (`Material.item` is `DeferredItem<? extends Item>`).
- **Done, PR 4:** TFMG fuels in the `thruster_fuel` data map (`ModDataMapProvider`): kerosene 0.8, LPG 0.7, gasoline 0.6, diesel 0.5, all 3 mB per tick, against Nebulite Propellant 1.0 at 2 mB per tick.
- **Done, Helm PR 1 (seat and the six axes):** `ship/HelmBlock` (GeckoLib, `geo/block/helm.geo.json` from the owner's chair model, `turn_180` plays on sitting), `HelmSeat` (an invisible no-gravity entity the pilot rides; it stores its Helm's block position, because on a ship the Helm's position is in the ship's plot, far from where the seat floats in the world), `HelmBlockEntity` (each tick: find the ship with `Ships.shipAt`, list its thrusters, `ThrustAllocator.throttles` from the pilot's `PilotInput`, set each thruster's commanded throttle, which beats redstone for that tick and the next so tick order does not matter), `ThrustAllocator` (pure: a thruster opens in proportion to how well its force and torque serve the request; translation keys ignore the torque they cause and rotation keys the force, which the holds of PR 2 will handle), `PilotInput` (six axes, 12 bits, `PilotInputPayload`), `ThrusterLike` (what the Helm needs of a thruster). The pilot's right is `forward x up` (Minecraft is left-handed: facing north, right is east). Client: `ClientPilotController` (movement keys plus Q/E roll and arrow keys for pitch and yaw, new `ModKeyMappings`), `HelmRenderer`. **Not yet:** the holds (RCS lock, dampener), the HUD, camera roll.
- **Done, Helm PR 2 (the holds):** `FlightSettings.rcsLock` (saved, synced, default on; `ToggleFlightSettingPayload.Setting.RCS_LOCK`, key X) beside the dampener (Z). Each Helm tick, `ThrustAllocator.holdRequest` asks for the opposite of the ship's velocity (dampener) and spin (RCS lock), in the ship frame (world velocity turned by the conjugate of `Ships.orientation`), but only on the axes the pilot is not using, so a key always wins; `ThrustAllocator.throttles(thrusters, frame, force, torque)` is the continuous entry the keys and the holds both feed, and the Helm takes the larger throttle of the two. Speeds under `HOLD_DEADBAND` (0.05) count as stopped; above it the request grows with the speed up to full at `HOLD_RAMP` (1 block/s or 1 rad/s), so a hold eases off as the ship comes to rest. The allocator scores a thruster by `along + turning` (signed), so one that serves one request while fighting the other stays shut; this is what lets the dampener and the lock run together. A ship can only hold the axes it has jets for: the holds test ship carries yaw, roll and fore-aft pairs; a real ship without roll jets keeps any roll it picks up.
- **Done, Helm PR 3 (pilot HUD and camera):** `HelmBlockEntity.Readout` grew `velocity` (along the Helm's forward, right, up; blocks/s), `spin` (roll right, nose up, yaw right; degrees/s) and an `Attitude` (`ship/Attitude`, pure: pitch, roll, compass heading from the ship's orientation and the Helm's axes); the Helm sends it to the pilot every tick (`HelmReadoutPayload`, clientbound, `clientHandler` pattern) and `ClientPilotController` keeps the latest. `client/hud/PilotHudLayer` (right of the hotbar: speed or why there is none, the two holds, fuel and energy across the thrusters, the thruster count with the twelve axis labels lit where the ship has reach; left of the hotbar: an artificial horizon box with heading/pitch/roll text). `client/camera/ShipCamera` adds a roll in `ViewportEvent.ComputeCameraAngles` so the view stays upright relative to the ship (`ship/ShipCameraMath`, pure: the ship's render-pose up from Sable's `ClientSubLevel.renderPose(partial)`, projected across the camera's line of sight; degenerate when looking along the ship's up, then the last roll is kept); photo mode owns the roll and is left alone, the orbit camera only adds yaw and pitch so it still works. `Ships.shipAt(Level, BlockPos)` finds a ship on either side. The Helm peripheral gained `getVelocity`, `getSpin`, `getAttitude`. **Seat fix:** the seat stays a world entity and `HelmSeat.tick` moves it to the Helm's world position (`worldSpot`: the Helm's plot position through the ship's `logicalPose`) every tick; before, it stayed behind in the world and the Helm lost its pilot as soon as the ship moved. Do not tag it `sable:retain_in_sub_level`: that keeps it (and the pilot) in the ship's plot, where the pilot sees the plot, not the world. **Not checked by eye:** the roll sign and the horizon's direction were pinned by unit tests from vanilla's `Camera.setRotation` maths, not seen in a client.
**Moving stations and docking (interview 2026-10-31, owner's answers)**
- **Scope:** moving stations first, then the portal-crossing ship spike (a PR if it works); the owner's own airship kit waits for its own interview.
- **Station Core** block: built into a hull, sneak-use assembles everything connected (Sable's own flood fill over the 18 face-and-edge neighbours, `SubLevelAssemblyHelper.gatherConnectedBlocks` + `assembleBlocks`) into one Sable ship named after the core (the item's custom name, or CC `setName`); sneak-use again sets it down as blocks. Also by redstone later and by CC `assemble()`/`disassemble()` now. Station features in the first slice: name and HUD markers, respawn at the station (a Backup Core aboard), a Replicator bonus within range, a shared station chest.
- **Docking Clamp** block: a visiting ship's block touching the clamp's face, with the clamp powered, is merged into the station's body (the visitor's blocks move into the station's plot); release splits it back into its own ship. Merge-while-clamped was chosen over a physics joint or freezing the visitor.
- **Portal crossing** (spike, then PR): a ship crosses only if its bounds fit the gate; the seated pilot and people standing on it come along; it keeps its velocity through the matching portal.
- **Sable facts (javap, 2.0.6):** no disassembly API in Sable: Aeronautics' nested "Simulated" jar has `SimAssemblyHelper.disassembleSubLevel(Level, SubLevel, BlockPos anchorInPlot, BlockPos target, Rotation, boolean)` (quarter turns about Y only), now on the compile classpath through `extractCreateLibs` (`createJar` on the Aeronautics wrapper). No merge API: moving blocks from one ship into another's plot is `disassembleSubLevel` with a target inside the other plot (Simulated's Merging Glue does this). Splitting is automatic on block removal (`SubLevelHeatMapManager`) and explicit through `assembleBlocks` from inside a plot, which inherits the parent's pose and velocity. Nothing crosses dimensions: a crossing recreates the ship (copy its plot blocks into a new plot in the target level, carry pose, velocity and the people aboard). Joints exist (`PhysicsPipeline.addConstraint`, `Fixed/Free/Rotary/GenericConstraintConfiguration`). Per-ship custom data: `ServerSubLevel.getUserDataTag()/setUserDataTag` (saved as `user_data`). Who is aboard: `Sable.HELPER.getTrackingOrVehicleSubLevel(Entity)`.
- **Done, stations PR 1 (the Station Core):** `station/StationCoreBlock` + `StationCoreBlockEntity` (a `BaseContainerBlockEntity` chest of 27 slots opened by right-click, `ChestMenu`; its own `stationName` from the item's custom name, written back on break; a stable `id`; the owner), `station/StationAssembly` (assemble: gather + `assembleBlocks`, name the ship, mark it in user data; set down: must be level within 10 degrees, snapped to the nearest quarter turn (`station/StationMath`, pure), refused when any landing block is not air or replaceable, then `disassembleSubLevel`), `station/Stations` (every loaded core registers itself; once a second the level's `StationList` attachment (names, ids, world positions, assembled) is rebuilt and synced; `replicatorSteps(player)` gives `station.replicatorSpeed` ticks of Replicator progress per tick within `station.range` blocks of a station), `client/hud/StationMarkerLayer` (edge-pinned name and distance for the three nearest stations beyond 24 blocks), respawn at a Backup Core aboard a station transforms through the ship's pose (`BackupCores`), CC `StationPeripheral` (getName/setName/isAssembled/assemble/disassemble/getPosition/getFilledSlots). Placeholder textures `station_core_top/side/bottom`.
- **Done, stations PR 2 (the Docking Clamp):** `station/DockingClampBlock` (faces any of six ways) + `DockingClampBlockEntity`: every 5 ticks while powered and empty it looks for a ship with a block in the cell in front of its face (the cell's world point, mapped into each other ship's frame with `transformPositionInverse`); the visitor must be level and within 10 degrees of a quarter turn relative to the station (`StationMath.yawResidualDegrees`), every landing cell in the station's plot must be air, then `SimAssemblyHelper.disassembleSubLevel(level, visitor, touchingBlock, frontCell, rotation, true)` moves its blocks into the station's plot (the emptied visitor is removed by Sable) and the landing positions, the visitor's name and its user data are recorded. Unpowered after docking by power (or sneak-use, or CC `release()`), `SubLevelAssemblyHelper.assembleBlocks` from inside the station's plot makes the recorded blocks their own ship again, with the station's pose and velocity, named as before. CC `DockingClampPeripheral`: isDocked/getDockedName/getStatus/dock/release. Placeholder textures `docking_clamp_face/side/back`.
- **Done, stations PR 3 (ships through the Atmospheric Portal):** `earth/PortalPanes` (a saved level attachment of pane positions kept by `AtmosphericPortalBlock.onPlace/onRemove`), `earth/ShipPortalTravel` (every 2 ticks: for each ship whose world bounds overlap a portal's plane and whose velocity goes through it from the side it is on, `cross`), `earth/ShipGate` (pure: the quarter turns between the way a ship leaves one gate and comes out the other, the ship's world extents from its block bounds and pose, the fit check with half a block of slack). Crossing: outward it must fit the gate (else it bumps with a message and a 5 s cooldown in its user data); `PortalTravel.ensureGate` makes Earth's gate and the exit link is set as for a walking traveller; the ship's blocks are copied as world blocks beyond the far gate, turned by the ship's own nearest quarter turn plus the gate turn, block entities carried by NBT, assembled with Sable's `assembleBlocks`, named and given its user data; two ticks later (the body exists then) it is turned to its exact orientation and given its turned velocity and spin. The pilot (riding a `HelmSeat` whose Helm is on the ship) and anyone Sable tracks as standing on it (`Sable.HELPER.getTrackingOrVehicleSubLevel`) are teleported to their spots and the pilot is re-seated at the Helm. Back from Earth it uses the remembered exit (or the deep-space spawn). The old ship is removed before the new one is built. On Earth an arriving ship lands on the ground; in space it keeps flying.
- **Done, PR 2 (Earthfall and the Aeronautics parts):** milestone `earthfall` (`Milestones.EARTHFALL`, order 60) with the new criterion `dimension_visited` (`MilestoneCriteria.DimensionVisited`); `ReplicatorData.dimensionsVisited` is recorded by `Replicator.noteDimension` on every dimension change and login (`MilestoneContext.dimensionsVisited`). Replicator recipes `replicator/aeronautics_<id>` for every Aeronautics block and item (all 16 envelope colours and their encased shafts, propellers, bearings, vent, burner, levitite and pearlescent levitite, the potato cannon, the aviator's goggles, end stone powder; Levitite Blend is a fluid, not an item), category Blocks, gated by Earthfall, items looked up from the registry at datagen (Aeronautics loads in `runData`); placeholder dust costs. Three Create routes under the chain toggles: Carbon Composite Plate pressed to an Andesite Propeller, a Micro-Alloy Sheet pressed to a Steam Vent, Pure Silicon Dust and Fine Carbon Powder mixed (heated) to Levitite Blend. Tests: `EarthMilestoneGameTests` (stepping onto ruined Earth reaches Earthfall and it stays; the propeller recipe is LOCKED before and QUEUED after).
- **PR order:** (1) Chemical Thruster with Sable as a compile dependency, `ThrusterMath` (pure, unit-tested), the fuel data map with Nebulite Propellant, a redstone throttle, a CC peripheral, an Aeronautics GameTest (a fuelled, powered thruster speeds a ship up along its nozzle; no fuel or no redstone, no push); (2) Ion Thruster; (3) RCS block; (4) TFMG fuels; then the Helm interview; then the Propulsion spike. The owner gave the go for PRs 1 to 4.
