# Ruined Earth

Settled decisions, moved here from `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them current in the same PR as the change.

**Act III slice (2026-10-30 interview, owner's answers)**
- **Terrain:** custom generator (`earth/RuinedEarthChunkGenerator`), 1024 x 1024 planet, void outside (no vanilla per-dimension border in 1.21.1). Normal gravity on ruined_earth; thrusters will work as hover there (not built yet). Ashen Slag is the surface block.
- **Portal:** craftable frame blocks big enough for the Ship (about 12 x 5.6 inside), lit by an activation item; arriving builds a matching return gate at the landing area. **Sky:** one era for the whole dimension (ruined, healing, restored), set by a debug command first; blending by restoration percent comes later. **Spire:** a single block that pulses for free in the slice; the pulse is an expanding wave over a long period (about 15 s, config), rewrites blocks and biomes from a data-driven mapping, and resends chunks when it ends.
- **PR order:** (1) dimension (done), (2) sky eras (done, see PROGRESS.md), (3a) portal frame and lighting (done), (3b) portal travel and the return gate (done), (4) Spire pulse (done), then hover flight on Earth. **The Act III vertical slice is complete**; see the "Act III slice" blocks in `PROGRESS.md`.

**Hover flight on Earth (2026-10-30 interview, owner's answers; not built yet)**
- **Lift is real:** with gravity back on ruined Earth, weight acceleration = the body's `generic.gravity` times config `hover.weightFactor` (default 0.12 after measuring the real presets, see PROGRESS.md "Hover tuning"; first thought to be 0.6, one number to tune: a Mk I Drone has about 0.075 blocks/tick² of thrust against vanilla's 0.08). Zero-G is unchanged.
- **Weight comes first:** with the dampener on, thrust is spent cancelling weight (up to the total), and only the remainder steers. If thrust cannot cover weight, the body still falls but more slowly (partial lift) and does not steer. Dampener off: gravity applies in full.
- **Energy:** hover costs the usual thrust cost times the share of thrust spent on weight, times the body's energy multiplier. Heavier bodies therefore drain more with no extra constants. Low power mode has no thrust, so the body falls.
- **Fall damage on Earth is vanilla** (it is only cancelled in zero-G). It stays: see "Danger" in `docs/design/plans.md`.
- **Walk or fly:** a chassis flag `walks` (datapack field, default false, so future bodies can set it) marks bodies with legs. Only the Mech walks: it moves like a normal player until Space engages the thrusters (same hover model), and disengages on landing with no up-thrust held (my default, owner may change it). Drone, Ship and everything else always use thrusters on Earth.
- **PRs:** (1) pure hover physics, config, unit tests (done); (2) always-thruster bodies on Earth, server energy, HUD, GameTests (done; the unattended decisions are in `PROGRESS.md`); (3) Mech walking, `Chassis.walks`, the `engaged` input flag (done). **Hover flight on Earth is complete** (not yet seen in a client).

**Earth flight and airships (interview 2026-10-31, owner's answers)**
- **Thruster ships fly on ruined Earth too** (the owner: airships fit the theme less; smaller thruster ships should fly there); Aeronautics' parts fly there as well, and our own airship kit (envelope, gondola, propeller with our art) is a later slice with its own interview.
- **Controls:** the Helm is for thruster ships, in space and on Earth; airships stay Create-flown (Aeronautics has no control blocks of its own: propellers spin from shafts, so throttle and steering are redstone, Create links and which propellers you power, which our thrusters also answer to). No Helm adapter for propellers or lift.
- **Getting there:** materials go through the Atmospheric Portal as items and the ship is built on Earth; no ship crosses the portal yet (the portal-crossing spike stays a separate later step).
- **Weather:** wind drift only, on ships and the body; no damage, nothing grounded.
- **Aeronautics parts in the Replicator:** every Aeronautics block (all envelope colours included), unlocked by a milestone for first setting foot on ruined Earth; a few of them also get Create recipes ("not too many").
- **Done, PR 1 (Earth flight physics and wind):** `dimension_physics/ruined_earth.json` states Sable's defaults for Earth (gravity 11 down, pressure 1, drag 0.09). `earth/EarthWind` is a pure field (sines over position and time, horizontal, never above the config top speed `earth.windSpeed`, default 1.5 blocks/s, 0 off; swings round in a minute; identical on client and server); `FlightPhysics.step` takes the wind and moves the body's horizontal velocity `WIND_COUPLING` (0.02) of the way to the air's each tick, after thrust and braking, so a key or the dampener always wins; `ServerFlightHandler` allows the wind on top of top speed; `earth/WindDrift` nudges every ship over Earth the same way through `RigidBodyHandle` each server tick (Sable's own `registerWindProvider` is read by nothing in Sable, Aeronautics or Propulsion). `RuinedEarthShipGameTests` (Aeronautics suite): a nozzle-down Chemical Thruster lifts its ship against gravity, an Aeronautics wooden propeller on a creative motor pushes its ship through the air, a free hull is carried the way the wind blows. Test-ship facts: Sable weighs blocks by tag (stone 2, redstone block 4, most blocks 1), so hulls must be symmetric about the thrust line or they tip; creative motors run at 16 rpm until `ScrollValue` is set (max 256); Sable's air pressure decays with height from the generator's sea level (-63 on ruined Earth, so about 0.35 at y 200). `AeronauticsGameTests`' helpers are package-private for it.

**Finishing the Spire (stage 2 of "Next four stages", built unattended from 2026-10-31, one slice at a time)**
- **Done, PR 1 (Bio-Nutrient Slurry):** `ModFluids.BIO_NUTRIENT_SLURRY` with its bucket; Create basin recipe `create/terraform/bio_nutrient_slurry` (250 mB Purified Water + Fine Carbon Powder + Ashen Slag, unheated, gives 250 mB), toggle `bioSlurry`.
- **Done, PR 2 (the tower):** `terraforming_spire` keeps its id and is now the tower's controller (`SpireBlock`, a `HorizontalKineticBlock` facing the tower, shaft on its back; old Spires load facing north with no tower and keep their count and any running pulse). `SpireTower` (pure): a 5 x 5 base with the Spire in the middle of the near edge, the frame being the base's outer row plus four corner pillars 9 tall, the column the 3 x 3 inside. `SpireAssembly` follows `LaunchComplex`: sneak-use assembles (frame cells in `#void_reclaimer:spire_frame`, column free, filled with invisible `spire_column` blocks), a broken frame sets it down, a missing column block grows back. Pulses sweep out from the tower's centre. A wrench leaves an assembled Spire alone. Placeholder model `geo/entity/spire_tower`, drawn by `client/render/SpireTowerRenderer`.
- **Done, PR 3 (sectors and the sky):** `earth/Sectors` (pure): the 4 x 4 grid of 256-block squares, numbered `row * 4 + column` from the north-west. `earth/SectorMap`: one bit per sector, a saved, synced attachment on the Earth level (`EARTH_SECTORS`), so a client only has it while on Earth. `earth/EarthRestoration` is the one place that changes it: it moves the sky era on (`SkyEra.forSectors`: ruined at 0, healing at 1 to 15, restored at 16) and posts `SectorRestoredEvent`. A tower's pulse sweeps out from the tower but stays inside the sector holding the tower's centre, wherever in it the tower stands (`Sectors.reach` sets its radius, so config `spireRadius` is gone), and marks that sector when it ends; a tower refuses off the planet and over a restored sector. `SkyLook.in(level)` blends the look with the fraction restored (ruined at 0, healing at one half, restored at 1), which is also the smog fading per sector; an era set with `/void_reclaimer sky era set` differs from `forSectors`, so it shows as it is until the next sector. `/void_reclaimer sky sectors [count]` reads the count or marks the first sectors without touching the ground.
- **Done, PR 4 (the cost):** firing checks the cost and starts a charge; the charge needs the shaft turning (config `minRpm`; `chargeTicks` at 64 RPM, faster with speed; it waits while the shaft is still) and pays at the end, all or nothing, then the wave leaves. One pulse burns one item from `#void_reclaimer:spire/catalysts`, 8 buckets from the tag `spire/water` and 4 from `spire/slurry` (config `catalysts`, `water`, `slurry`, tanks `tank`). Automation only fills (`items()`, `fluids()`; capabilities on any face); a right-click with a catalyst or a bucket loads it by hand. 32 SU per RPM. CC: `isCharging`, `getCharge`, `getCost`, `getStock`.
- **Done, PR 5 (Acid Drizzle):** `earth/EarthAtmosphere` (pure): strength is 0 in a restored sector, otherwise `1 - fraction restored`; it always falls, with no weather cycle. `earth/AcidDrizzle`: once a second a body with nothing solid above its head (a heightmap, so a roof counts at once) loses `earth.acidWearPerMinute` (0.003 at full strength) on every section through `Wear.add`, so it is repairable, never breaks a section and spares creative. Config `earth.acidDrizzle` switches off both the wear and the drops. Client: `client/render/AcidDrizzleClient` draws `acid_drop` particles and plays `acid_drizzle` (rain as a stand-in). Bodies only: remote shells do not wear yet.
- **Done, PR 6 (the Planetary Grid):** `earth/PlanetaryGridItem`, one to a stack, Replicator Tools tab behind Earthfall. Level attachments only reach clients in that level, so the item carries the map itself: `inventoryTick` copies `EarthRestoration.map` into the `planet_sectors` component once a second when it differs, which works from deep space with no packet. `client/hud/PlanetaryGridLayer`, shown for anything with that component in either hand: the 4 x 4 map at the right edge, north up, restored cells green, the player's own sector outlined on Earth, "n of 16" under it. **The stage is done:** the tower, the cost, restoration, the atmosphere and the map.
